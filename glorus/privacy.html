GLORuS privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

GLORuS is a private assistant run by one household on its own computer, for family members the owner adds. This policy explains what it does with Google data.

What GLORuS accesses

  • Your Google name, email address and profile picture, to sign you in and show who you are.
  • Gmail: reading messages you ask about, and writing drafts in your Drafts folder. GLORuS never sends email.
  • Google Calendar: your list of calendars, your events, and adding events after you confirm.

Where it goes

  • Your Google sign-in and anything GLORuS saves (chats, tasks, notes, memories) stay on the household's computer.
  • To answer a question, GLORuS sends the parts of your email or calendar that the question needs to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the reply. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • GLORuS's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google in GLORuS's Settings at any time, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask the household's owner to remove you; your data in GLORuS is then deleted.
